# Ahmed's taste: what gets requested or skipped

The daily run reads this before choosing items and rewrites it from the review page's decisions (`decisions` collection). Patterns marked *hypothesis* come from decisions without reasons; they turn into rules only once reasons confirm them.

## Hard rules (confirmed)
- Iraq only, cash on delivery. Dropship-suitable items only.
- Exclude clothing and sized items, liquids and cosmetics, fragile items.
- Air freight only: volume weight (L×W×H ÷ 5000) ≤ 2 kg.
- Iraq price = 3 × landed cost, within 35,000–75,000 IQD.
- No profit floor at selection. The ghost test budget averages about $50 per item.

## Batch 2026-11-22: 22 reviewed, 8 requested (36%)
Requested: car dent puller, vacuum storage bags with pump, rechargeable hand warmers, motion-sensor headlamp, cordless bottle warmer, green laser level, telescopic sink rack, cat projector toy.
Skipped: air duster, anti-glare visor, car vacuum, cheese grater, dash cam, door seal, flashlight, galaxy projector, screwdriver cube, titanium board, utensil holder, wave lamp, bug zapper, heated throw.

*Hypotheses (need reasons to confirm):*
- Battery items are fine: 6 of 8 requests have batteries.
- Problem-solving gadgets for personal use, travel, baby and pets get requested.
- Already-common items get skipped: flashlight, galaxy projector, car vacuum.
- Car accessories are mixed: dent puller in; visor, dash cam and car vacuum out.
- Low-value kitchen accessories get skipped: utensil holder, cheese grater. The titanium board was skipped despite the TikTok trend.
